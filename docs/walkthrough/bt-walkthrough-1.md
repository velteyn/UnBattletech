# Crescent Hawk's Inception Walkthrough

*Wars in the Inner Sphere*

*Sourced from a BBS a long, long time ago with a few corrections and additions (plus a complete map of Pacifica) from Rocqueforte O'Leary and Matt McLaine. Map Room picture by Anonymous, Copy Protection picture by Matt McLaine.*

*Portions by Joshua Mandel and Andy Backer. Cache Map by Jonathan Schattke.*

---

As the game begins, you find yourself in the Pacifica Training School, and your only immediate objective is to complete the training necessary to graduate as a Mech Warrior in the Lyran Commonwealth Armed Forces.

Explore the area. You start out opposite the Citadel, where you should meet Katrina, and take gunnery classes. Next door is Comstar, where the stock market can be played. To your far left you will find your barracks. Southeast of the barracks is the entrance to the Mech training center.

Other buildings you can enter are:
- Weapons shop
- Armor shop
- Lounge
- Mechit-Lube

Most of the time, when entering a lounge or other building and attempting to talk with somebody, nobody will be interested in talking to you. In order to increase your opportunities, hang around outside a building. When you see somebody enter the building, follow them inside, and immediately try to talk.

---

# MONEY

Probably the biggest factor in the whole game is money.

No money:
- no skill
- no mech
- no chance

Every five minutes or so your account is boosted by fifteen credits as your parents send you more allowance.

Initial strategy:
1. Invest in a low risk account
2. Move onto the intermediate one
3. Wait several hours

Expected result:
- Around 350,000 credits

---

# RECOMMENDED STOCKS

Only two stocks are worth your while:

- **NASHAN DIVERSIFIED (NasDiv)**
- **BAKER PHARMACEUTICALS (BakPhar)**

## NasDiv
A steady performer suitable for long-term investment.

## BakPhar
A speculative stock that can make you rich very quickly if timed properly.

Suggested strategy:
- Put most money into NasDiv
- Put a smaller amount into BakPhar
- Save frequently
- Watch the market constantly

BakPhar can also crash to zero instantly.

### Additional note
**DEFIANCE INDUSTRIES OF HESPERUS (DefHes)** is another safe long-term investment.

# EXPLORING THE COMPOUND

After investing:
- Explore
- Rest
- Visit shops
- Train

Return frequently to check your stocks.

Alternative approach:
- Leave the game running for several hours
- Return later to collect your fortune

---

# TRAINING SCHOOL

Your main goal is learning how to operate Mechs.

Minimum:
- Six lessons

Important skills:
- Rifle
- Machine gun

Take an apprenticeship at the Mechit-Lube:
- Expensive
- But allows mech repairs

Combat training advice:
- Keep moving
- Avoid overheating
- Focus on speed

Medical skill is also valuable.

---

## Important Note

If you proceed directly north after leaving the compound and starport, you can recruit the doctor later and may never need:
- weapon skills
- medical skills

Reason:
A personal SRM launcher can kill nearly any human enemy in one shot.

---

# WEAPONRY

## Early Game
Use:
- Machine gun

Cheap but weak.

## Mid/Late Game
Recommended:
- Laser weapon
- SRM launcher (best option)

### SRM Advantages
- One-shot kills humans
- Damages mechs
- Infinite ammo
- Rarely misses

Do NOT:
- Fight heavy mechs on foot with handheld SRMs

Recommended armor:
- 40-point armor

Also buy:
- MedKit
- Mapper

Later:
- Upgrade mechs at Speed Shops

---

# MECH ETIQUETTE

Always customize your mech:
- Extra lasers
- Extra armor

Useful advice:
- Kicking is powerful
- Always face enemy mechs
- Protect your rear armor

---

# THE INVASION

During the 6th or 7th training mission:
- Kuritan forces invade
- Four Jenners appear

Goal:
- Escape to Starport

## Escape Method 1
Use a Locust:
- Head west
- Leave the city

## Escape Method 2
Use a Chameleon:
- Re-enter building
- Exit west after walls collapse

Sometimes neither works.

---

# STARPORT

Head north to Starport.

First objectives:
- Remove your uniform
- Reinvest stocks
- Visit the Inaugural Hall

Optional:
- Fight in Arena for money

Possible purchases:
- Holodisc player
- Mapper

---

## Arena Exploit

You can:
- Rent an Arena mech
- Blow open the western stands
- Escape with the mech

Consequence:
- Starport bans you permanently

There is also a tiny chance to obtain the only UrbanMech in the game.

---

# REX PEARCE & THE CRESCENT HAWKS

At the Inaugural Hall you meet **Rex Pearce**.

He:
- Gives you a box from your father
- Joins your mission
- Provides a Commando mech

Mission:
- Gather Crescent Hawks members
- Find hidden mech cache
- Contact Katrina Steiner

The holocard containing cache information becomes damaged during an attack.

---

# NOTE ON TRAITORS

Some recruitable characters are secretly enemy agents.

Warning signs:
- "Something's not right"
- "He's nervous"

If discovered:
- Kill the traitor
- Reload if necessary

Some game versions contain only one copy of certain characters, making reloads essential.

---

# THE JAIL & THE UNDERCOVER AGENT

Travel east-northeast from Starport.

Possible outcomes:
- Recruit ally
- Discover traitor

If an agent is present:
- Services may appear closed
- NPCs may refuse conversation

You can expose agents by bringing them into combat.

If you lack a mech:
- Steal one from the jail garage

---

# FINDING THE OTHER HAWKS, DR. TELLHIM & THE CACHE

Two methods exist to view the damaged holocard:

1. Return to the Citadel barracks
2. Use the Mayor's holocard viewer southwest of Starport

You then learn:
- Find Dr. Tellhim in the northwest

---

## Recruiting Allies

### Doctor
Found in hospitals.

Recommended:
- MedKit
- Field Surgery
- Medical Seminar

### Tech
Found at Mechit-Lubes.

Recommended:
- Additional tech training
- Apprenticeship

Benefits:
- Salvages mech parts
- Improves repairs

Located near:

- Excellent Tech

- Good Pilot

He reveals:
- Cache location on island southeast

Coordinates:
- 39/60 x 55/60

# THE COMPUTER TERMINALS

Inside the cache:
- Maze of doors
- Computer terminals
- Color-coded key system

Each door requires:
- One red code
- One blue code
- One yellow code

You must open:
- ELEVEN doors
- 33 code combinations total

Important:
- Keycard stores only one code per color at a time

Move south along the western corridor where the Y21 machine is located.

Continue through the apparently blocked corridor.

Reward:
- Hidden Phoenix Hawk LAM

1. Return upstairs

3. Activate transmitter room
